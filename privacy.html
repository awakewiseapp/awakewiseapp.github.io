AwakeWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short: AwakeWise collects no personal data, has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and never connects to the internet.

What AwakeWise does

AwakeWise is a menu bar app that keeps your Mac awake. It does this with Apple's standard power-management feature, and only while you have asked it to.

Information we collect

None. AwakeWise does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any information about you or how you use your Mac. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code, and it makes no network connections.

Information that stays on your Mac

  • Settings — your choices (for example the apps that turn AwakeWise on, or your default duration) are saved in AwakeWise's own preferences on your Mac.
  • Calendar — only if you turn on “During Calendar Events”, AwakeWise asks for calendar access and reads the times of your current events to decide whether to keep your Mac awake. Event details are read on your Mac, are not stored by AwakeWise and never leave your Mac. You can withdraw access at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars.
  • Running apps — to offer “Until a Task Finishes” and “While These Apps Are Running”, AwakeWise looks at which apps are open on your Mac. This information is used only at that moment and is not recorded.
  • Downloads, folders and activity — only while you use “Downloads Finish”, “A Folder Stops Changing”, “Transfers Finish” or “The Mac Is Idle”, AwakeWise checks the names and sizes of files in your Downloads folder or the folder you chose, the Mac's total network traffic, or overall processor load, to tell whether work is still going on. Files are never opened, copied or sent, and nothing is recorded.
  • Battery level — if Battery Saver is on, AwakeWise reads your battery level and whether you're plugged in. It is not recorded.
  • Notifications — if you allow them, AwakeWise shows a local notification when a task finishes. No notification is sent through any server.

Children

AwakeWise does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
