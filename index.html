Stay awake until it's done

Tell AwakeWise what to wait for and it keeps your Mac awake until the work is done. No timer to run out halfway through.

  • Until downloads finish, network transfers finish, or your Mac goes idle — no need to know which app is doing the work
  • Until a folder stops changing — perfect for exports and renders
  • Until an app quits — a video export, an installer, a game update
  • Until a Terminal command or script says it's finished
  • Until a time — "keep awake until 5:30"
  • Or simply 1 hour, 2 hours, or indefinitely

On automatically

Set it once and forget it.

  • On a schedule — for example weekdays 9 to 6
  • Whenever chosen apps are open — Keynote, a video player, your editor
  • During calendar meetings with invitees or a video link, so the screen never dims mid-call
  • Let the display sleep while the Mac keeps working on downloads and renders
  • Battery Saver steps aside when your MacBook runs low
  • Shortcuts and Siri actions, plus a Control Center toggle on macOS 26 or later
  • Countdown in the menu bar and an optional "done" notification

Built for long jobs and AI coding tools

Any script or tool can tell AwakeWise "keep the Mac awake until I'm done". Each job has its own name, so several can run at once, and none of them will switch off a session you started yourself.

open -g "awakewise://wait?label=Export" && ./export.sh
open -g "awakewise://done?label=Export"

Add the same two lines as hooks in your AI coding assistant and your Mac stays awake while it works, then sleeps when it's finished. All commands
